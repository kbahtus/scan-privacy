# Scan: privacy note

Scan is a private tool for wholesale buying, used only by its owner and the owner's buying team. It is not offered to the public.

**What Scan can see in Google.** When the owner signs in with Google, Scan asks for one permission only: Google's "drive.file" permission. With it, Scan can see and change only the files Scan creates itself. It uses that to keep backup copies of its own data in the owner's own Google Drive. Scan cannot see any other file in that Drive. Separately, a read-only Google account belonging to Scan can open only the team folders the owner has shared with it.

**What Scan does not do.** Scan does not sell, rent or share any data. It does not use Google data for advertising, and it does not send Google data to anyone except back to the owner. Scan's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

**Removing access.** The owner can remove Scan's access at any time at myaccount.google.com, under "Third-party apps and services".

**Contact.** Questions go to the support email shown on Scan's Google sign-in screen.
